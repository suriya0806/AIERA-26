ABOUT

AIERA 2K26 is the first symposium organized by the Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML) Department of Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering. The symposium brings together students, innovators and technology enthusiasts to explore the rapidly evolving world of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

01 // AI & ML

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

AI and Machine Learning are transforming the way people solve problems, automate tasks, understand data and build intelligent applications. AIERA 2K26 provides a platform to learn, experiment and compete through practical technical challenges, creative activities and collaborative experiences.

02 // VISION

Learn • Innovate • Compete

From intelligent systems and data-driven solutions to emerging generative AI, AIERA creates an environment where participants can share ideas, develop skills, challenge themselves and experience the future of technology.
